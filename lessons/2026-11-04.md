**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月4日**
**漢字**
- 盛 — 音読み: セイ、ジョウ / 訓読み: も-る、さか-る、さか-ん — to heap up, to serve into a dish; to flourish. In the 麹室（こうじむろ） it names the step where the growing kōji is portioned out into small trays: 盛り（もり）.
- 蓋 — 音読み: ガイ / 訓読み: ふた — lid, cover. In sake it appears in 麹蓋（こうじぶた）, the shallow cedar tray the kōji grows in (the name likely comes from its lid-like shape).

**ことば**
- 盛り（もり） — about a day after 引き込み（ひきこみ）, the kōji is divided into 麹蓋（こうじぶた） or larger boxes so heat and moisture can be controlled in small portions. Same idea as 盛る（もる）, "to dish out."
- 麹蓋（こうじぶた） — a small, shallow tray of 杉（すぎ） (cedar), holding only a little rice. It's the most hands-on method (麹蓋法（こうじぶたほう）) and many breweries still use it for their top-grade kōji.
- 仲仕事（なかしごと） — the "middle work," several hours after 盛り（もり）: the kōji is mixed and spread more thinly to release the heat the mold is now producing.
- 仕舞仕事（しまいしごと） — the "final work," several hours after 仲仕事（なかしごと）: one last mix, spreading the kōji thin, often raked into ridges and furrows so it cools and dries evenly.

**例文**
盛り（もり）で麹（こうじ）を麹蓋（こうじぶた）に小分け（こわけ）し、仲仕事（なかしごと）と仕舞仕事（しまいしごと）で薄（うす）く広（ひろ）げて熱（ねつ）を逃（に）がす。
||In the mori step the kōji is divided among small cedar trays, and during the middle and final work it is spread thin to let the heat escape.||
