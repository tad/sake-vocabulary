**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月27日**
**漢字**
- 杜 — 音読み: ト、ズ / 訓読み: もり — a grove, especially the sacred wood around a shrine. In sake it lives almost entirely in 杜氏, the title of the master brewer. Where the word came from isn't settled: one theory points to 杜康（とこう）, the legendary Chinese inventor of alcohol; another to 刀自（とじ）, the old word for the woman who ran a household, from the days when women brewed.
- 蔵 — 音読み: ゾウ / 訓読み: くら — storehouse; the brewery itself. It's the 蔵 in 酒蔵, and also the ゾウ in 貯蔵 that you met during storage week.

**ことば**
- 杜氏（とうじ） — the master brewer, responsible for the whole season's brewing and for the crew. Traditionally they came from farming and fishing regions to work through the winter, and each region's guild has its own lineage and style: 南部杜氏（なんぶとうじ, Iwate）, 越後杜氏（えちごとうじ, Niigata）, 但馬杜氏（たじまとうじ, Hyōgo）and others.
- 蔵人（くらびと） — the brewery workers under the 杜氏. In court history the same characters are read くろうど (a palace official), but in a brewery it's always くらびと.
- 蔵元（くらもと） — the brewery owner, or the owning family and its company. The 蔵元 runs the business; the 杜氏 runs the brewing. Traditionally they were two different people.
- 三役（さんやく） — the three senior posts under the 杜氏: 頭（かしら, the foreman and second-in-command）, 麹屋（こうじや, in charge of kōji）and 酛屋（もとや, in charge of the yeast starter）.
- 蔵元杜氏（くらもととうじ） — an owner who is also the master brewer. As the seasonal guild brewers age out, more and more small breweries are going this way, often with a younger family member who trained at a university or the national research institute.

**例文**
最近は蔵元杜氏の蔵が増え、蔵元自らが蔵人を率（ひき）いて、三役の仕事にも目を配っている。
||These days there are more breweries run by an owner who is also the master brewer, and the owner personally leads the brewery workers while keeping an eye on the work of the three senior posts.||
