**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月23日**

**漢字**
- 杜 — 音読み: ト、ズ / 訓読み: もり — grove, especially the sacred wood around a shrine. In sake it appears almost only in 杜氏（とうじ）, the master brewer, where it is read with a long vowel: とう.
- 蔵 — 音読み: ゾウ / 訓読み: くら — storehouse. In sake, 蔵 means the brewery itself: brewers say 「うちの蔵」 for "our brewery," and it heads a whole family of words like 酒蔵（さかぐら）.

**ことば**
- 酒蔵（さかぐら） — a sake brewery, both the building and the business. Often shortened to just 蔵.
- 蔵元（くらもと） — the brewery owner: the owning family, and by extension the company. It is the 蔵元 who decides what kind of sake the brewery makes and sells.
- 杜氏（とうじ） — the master brewer, fully responsible for the brewing and the quality of the sake. Traditionally a seasonal worker, not the owner. One common theory traces the word to 刀自（とじ）, the old word for the woman who ran a household, since brewing was once women's work.
- 蔵人（くらびと） — the brewery workers under the 杜氏. Traditionally they came with the 杜氏 from farming villages as winter 出稼ぎ（でかせぎ）, seasonal work away from home. (The same kanji read くろうど means an old imperial court official, a different word.)
- 蔵元杜氏（くらもととうじ） — an owner who is also the master brewer. As the old guild brewers, such as 南部杜氏（なんぶとうじ）, 越後杜氏（えちごとうじ） and 但馬杜氏（たじまとうじ）, grow older, more small breweries are run this way.

**例文**
冬（ふゆ）になると、杜氏は蔵人を連（つ）れて酒蔵に入り、春の甑倒し（こしきだおし）まで蔵元のもとで酒を造（つく）った。
||When winter came, the master brewer would bring his brewery workers to the brewery and make sake for the owner until the last steaming of the season in spring.||
