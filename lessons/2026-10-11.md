**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月11日**
**漢字**
- 破 — 音読み: ハ / 訓読み: やぶ-る、やぶ-れる — to break, tear. In the 麹室, mold "breaking into" the rice is written 破精（はぜ）: the white patches where 麹菌 has taken hold.
- 突 — 音読み: トツ / 訓読み: つ-く — to thrust, pierce. It gives its name to 突き破精, koji whose mold pierces deep into the grain.
**ことば**
- 仕舞仕事（しまいしごと） — the final round of work on the koji, roughly 30-odd hours in. The koji is spread thinner and furrowed to shed heat and moisture. It follows 仲仕事（なかしごと）, the middle round.
- 出麹（でこうじ） — taking the finished koji out of the room, about two days after 引き込み. The word also means the finished koji itself.
- 破精込み（はぜこみ） — how deeply the mold has penetrated into the grain. Brewers judge koji on this as much as on how much surface it covers.
- 突き破精（つきはぜ） — koji with mold in scattered spots on the surface but reaching deep inside. Its moderate, steady enzyme power makes it the classic koji for 吟醸.
- 総破精（そうはぜ） — koji with mold covering the whole grain and penetrating deeply. Its strong enzymes give fuller, richer sake, and it is often used for 酒母.
**例文**
吟醸用の麹は突き破精を目指し、仕舞仕事のあと、破精込みを確かめてから出麹する。
||For ginjo koji, the brewer aims for mold that grows in spots but runs deep. After the final round of koji-room work, they check how far the mold has penetrated, then take the finished koji out of the room.||
