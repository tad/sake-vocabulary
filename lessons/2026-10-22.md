**漢字**
- 熟 — 音読み: ジュク / 訓読み: う-れる — ripe; to ripen, mature. Stored sake isn't just waiting, it's ripening, and this is the kanji for it: 熟成（じゅくせい）, 長期熟成酒（ちょうきじゅくせいしゅ）.
- 呑 — 音読み: ドン / 訓読み: の-む — to swallow, drink (a more colorful variant of 飲む). In the kura it names the tap at the base of a storage tank, 呑口（のみくち）, and the summer tasting below.
**ことば**
- 熟成（じゅくせい） — maturation, aging. Most sake rests in tank for months after the first 火入れ so rough edges round off; aged for years it becomes 長期熟成酒 or 古酒（こしゅ）, deepening in color toward amber.
- 蛇管（じゃかん） — coiled pipe ("snake pipe") used for pasteurizing: sake flows through a coil sitting in hot water and comes out at about 60-65°C. Many kura now use plate heat exchangers instead, but the word is still everyday kura talk.
- 呑み切り（のみきり） — summer tasting of stored sake: each tank's tap is opened and a sample drawn to check how it is maturing and whether anything has gone wrong (such as 火落ち). Traditionally a big event around June-August, often with outside technical advisors invited.
- 老香（ひねか） — stale, heavy aged off-aroma (think pickled vegetables, burnt sugar, sometimes a sulfury note) from heat, light, or too long in storage. Unpasteurized sake can develop a distinct version called 生老香（なまひね）.
**例文**
蛇管（じゃかん）で火入れした酒は、夏の呑み切りで熟成の具合と老香（ひねか）の有無を確かめる。
||Sake pasteurized through the coiled hot-water pipe is checked at the summer tank-tap tasting for how its maturation is coming along and whether any stale, aged off-aroma has appeared.||
