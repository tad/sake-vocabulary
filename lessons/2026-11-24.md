**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月24日**

**漢字**
- 役 — 音読み: ヤク、エキ / 訓読み: （常用の訓読みなし） — role, post, duty. In a brewery every 蔵人（くらびと） has a fixed post for the season, and the most senior posts are called 三役（さんやく）.
- 釜 — 音読み: フ (rarely used) / 訓読み: かま — a large iron cooking pot. In sake it is the pot that boils water under the 甑（こしき） to steam the rice, as in 和釜（わがま）, and it gives its name to the worker who runs it, the 釜屋（かまや）.

**ことば**
- 三役（さんやく） — the three senior posts directly under the 杜氏（とうじ）: 頭, 麹屋 and 酛屋. The same word is used in sumo and in politics for "the top three posts," so context tells you which one is meant.
- 頭（かしら） — the foreman and second-in-command, the 杜氏's right hand. He hands out the day's work, leads the 蔵人 and stands in for the 杜氏 when he is away. Note the reading: in a brewery this 頭 is かしら, not あたま.
- 麹屋（こうじや） — the worker in charge of making kōji in the 麹室（こうじむろ）. Also called 代師（だいし） in some regions. Don't confuse it with 種麹屋（たねこうじや）, the outside company that sells kōji spores.
- 酛屋（もとや） — the worker in charge of the yeast starter (酒母（しゅぼ）). Also called 酛廻り（もとまわり） in some breweries.
- 釜屋（かまや） — the worker who runs the 釜 and the steaming, so the first one up each morning. Post names and duties vary by guild and brewery, and today most breweries use a boiler instead of a real 釜, but the title survives.

**例文**
杜氏（とうじ）の下（もと）で、頭（かしら）が蔵人（くらびと）をまとめ、麹屋と酛屋はそれぞれの持ち場（もちば）を守（まも）り、釜屋は毎朝（まいあさ）一番に米を蒸（む）す。
||Under the master brewer, the foreman keeps the brewery workers together, the kōji maker and the starter maker each look after their own posts, and the steaming man is the first to steam the rice every morning.||
