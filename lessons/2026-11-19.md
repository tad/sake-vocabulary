**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月19日**

**漢字**
- 醸 — 音読み: ジョウ / 訓読み: かも-す — to brew, to ferment; figuratively, to bring about slowly (an atmosphere, a mood). It is the brewing kanji itself: 醸造（じょうぞう）, brewing, and 醸（かも）す, to brew sake. You have met it already inside 吟醸 and 速醸酛.

**ことば**
- 本醸造酒（ほんじょうぞうしゅ） — honjozo sake: rice, kōji and water plus a small amount of brewing alcohol (at most 10% of the polished rice's weight), with rice polished to 70% or less. Often light, dry and good warmed.
- 醸造アルコール（じょうぞうアルコール） — "brewing alcohol": neutral distilled alcohol, usually made from sugarcane molasses, added near the end of the moromi. It lightens the body and pulls aroma out of the mash, which is why many competition 大吟醸 use it.
- アル添（アルてん） — brewers' and drinkers' slang for adding alcohol (short for アルコール添加（てんか）); アル添酒 is any sake made this way. Neutral in the trade, though some fans use it dismissively.
- 柱焼酎（はしらじょうちゅう） — Edo-period practice of adding shōchū to the mash before pressing, to protect sake from spoilage and sharpen its flavor. It is the historical ancestor of today's アル添.

**例文**
本醸造酒に醸造アルコールを加（くわ）える「アル添」は、江戸時代（えどじだい）の柱焼酎にさかのぼる技法（ぎほう）で、すっきりと軽（かる）い味（あじ）を生（う）む。
||Adding brewing alcohol to honjozo sake, known for short as "alcohol addition," is a technique that goes back to the Edo-period custom of adding shōchū to the mash, and it gives the sake a clean, light taste.||
