**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月18日**
**漢字**
- 搾 — 音読み: サク / 訓読み: しぼ-る — to squeeze, press, wring; the moment the moromi becomes sake. You see it in 搾る（しぼる）to press, 圧搾（あっさく）mechanical pressing, and labels like 搾りたて（しぼりたて）freshly pressed.
- 槽 — 音読み: ソウ / 訓読み: ふね — tank, vat, trough; in a kura it is the long wooden or steel press box, read ふね. It gives 上槽（じょうそう）pressing and 槽搾り（ふなしぼり）, sake pressed the traditional way in that box.
**ことば**
- 上槽（じょうそう） — pressing: separating the finished moromi into clear sake and lees. The formal brewing term for the whole step; brewers also just say 搾り（しぼり）.
- 酒袋（さかぶくろ） — the cloth bag the moromi is poured into for pressing, once cotton, now usually synthetic. Bags are stacked in the ふね and pressed, or hung up to drip.
- 袋吊り（ふくろづり） — hanging the filled bags and letting sake drip out under its own weight, with no pressure. Slow and low-yield, it's used for competition and top ginjo bottlings; also called 雫取り（しずくどり）.
- 自動圧搾機（じどうあっさくき） — the accordion-style automatic press used in most kura today, widely called ヤブタ after its maker. Moromi is pumped between filter panels and squeezed by inflating air bladders.
- 酒粕（さけかす） — sake lees, the white cake left after pressing. Sold for cooking, 粕汁（かすじる）, and pickles; its share of the mash, the 粕歩合（かすぶあい）, tells you how hard the sake was pressed.
**例文**
大吟醸（だいぎんじょう）は袋吊りで雫（しずく）を集め、残りの醪（もろみ）は自動圧搾機で上槽し、酒粕は蔵の売店で売っている。
||For the daiginjo we hang the mash in cloth bags and collect the drops that fall on their own; the rest of the mash is pressed in the automatic press, and the sake lees are sold in the brewery shop.||
