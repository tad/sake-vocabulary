**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月9日**
**漢字**
- 醪 — 音読み: ロウ / 訓読み: もろみ — fermenting mash. Once the starter is finished, rice, kōji and water are added to it in the tank, and this main fermentation is the 醪（もろみ）. Today we leave the 酛（もと） behind and step into the tank. (It's often written in kana, もろみ, or as 諸味（もろみ）.)
- 添 — 音読み: テン / 訓読み: そ-える、そ-う — to add, to go along with. In the kura it names each addition of rice, kōji and water to the mash. Notice that it becomes ぞえ in compounds: 初添（はつぞえ）.

**ことば**
- 三段仕込み（さんだんじこみ） — three-stage mashing: rice, kōji and water go into the tank in three batches over four days, each one bigger than the last. Adding everything at once would dilute the yeast and the protective acidity; going step by step keeps the mash healthy. It's the standard method in almost every kura.
- 初添（はつぞえ） — the first addition, on day one. The starter goes into the tank with the first, smallest batch, kept fairly warm (around 12°C) so the yeast gets going. Often shortened to 添（そえ）.
- 踊り（おどり） — "the dance": day two, a rest day. Nothing is added, and the yeast multiplies. Brewers check the mash's condition before continuing.
- 仲添（なかぞえ） — the second, middle addition on day three, about twice the size of the first and cooler (around 9–10°C). Also just 仲（なか）.
- 留添（とめぞえ） — the third and final addition on day four: the largest batch, at the coldest temperature (around 7–8°C). Also just 留（とめ）. From here the 醪 ferments on its own for several weeks.

**例文**
初添（はつぞえ）の翌日（よくじつ）は踊り（おどり）といって仕込み（しこみ）を休み、酵母（こうぼ）が増えるのを待（ま）ってから仲添（なかぞえ）、留添（とめぞえ）と進めていく。
||The day after the first addition is the "dance": the brewers take a break from adding to the mash and wait for the yeast to multiply, then move on to the middle addition and finally the last one.||
