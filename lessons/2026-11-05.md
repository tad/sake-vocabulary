**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月5日**
**漢字**
- 破 — 音読み: ハ / 訓読み: やぶ-る、やぶ-れる — to break, tear. In the 麹室（こうじむろ） it describes mold "breaking into" the steamed rice: 破精（はぜ）, the white patches where 麹菌（こうじきん） has taken hold.
- 突 — 音読み: トツ / 訓読み: つ-く — to thrust, pierce. It names 突き破精（つきはぜ）, kōji whose mold pierces deep into the grain from just a few spots.

**ことば**
- 出麹（でこうじ） — taking the finished kōji out of the room, roughly two days after 引き込み（ひきこみ）. The word also means the finished kōji itself.
- 破精（はぜ） — the white, fuzzy areas where the mold has grown on and into a grain. Brewers look at both how much surface it covers and how deep it goes.
- 破精込み（はぜこみ） — how deeply the mold has penetrated into the grain. Deep 破精込み means the enzymes reach into the rice's core, where they'll do their work in the mash.
- 突き破精（つきはぜ） — kōji with mold in scattered spots on the surface but reaching deep inside. Its moderate, steady enzyme power makes it the classic kōji for 吟醸（ぎんじょう）.
- 総破精（そうはぜ） — kōji with mold covering the whole grain and penetrating deeply. Its strong enzymes give fuller, richer sake, and it is often used for 酒母（しゅぼ）.

**例文**
吟醸（ぎんじょう）用（よう）の麹（こうじ）は突き破精（つきはぜ）を、酒母（しゅぼ）用（よう）の麹（こうじ）は総破精（そうはぜ）を目指（めざ）し、破精込み（はぜこみ）を確（たし）かめてから出麹（でこうじ）する。
||Kōji for premium aromatic sake aims for deep spots of mold, kōji for the yeast starter aims for mold over the whole grain, and the brewers check how deeply the mold has penetrated before taking the kōji out of the room.||
