**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月25日**
**漢字**
- 称 — 音読み: ショウ / 訓読み: たた-える、とな-える — name, title; to praise. It's the 称 in 名称 (name, designation): a 特定名称酒 may use its grade name on the label only if it meets the legal conditions.
- 製 — 音読み: セイ / 訓読み: — — to make, manufacture. Shows up all over brewing: 製法 (production method), 製造 (manufacture), 製麹（せいぎく, koji making）.

**ことば**
- 本醸造酒（ほんじょうぞうしゅ） — premium sake made from rice, koji, water and a small amount of brewer's alcohol (no more than 10% of the rice weight), with rice polished to 70% or less. The alcohol is there to lift aroma and lighten the body, not to stretch volume. Its step up, 特別本醸造酒（とくべつほんじょうぞうしゅ）, needs 60% or less or a "special method", and the label must explain which.
- 製法品質表示基準（せいほうひんしつひょうじきじゅん） — the National Tax Agency's labeling standard for sake production methods and quality (notice issued 1989, in force 1990). It defines the eight special-designation grades and what labels may and must say.
- 麹歩合（こうじぶあい） — kōji ratio: the share of kōji rice in the total rice used. Special-designation sake must use at least 15%. Higher ratios generally mean more enzyme power and a richer, fuller sake.
- 等外米（とうがいまい） — ungraded rice: rice that hasn't passed inspection at grade 3 (3等) or better under the national agricultural products inspection. It can't be used for special-designation sake, only for 普通酒.
- 吟醸香（ぎんじょうか） — ginjo aroma: the fruity, floral bouquet (think banana or green apple) from slow, cold fermentation of highly polished rice. Main drivers are the esters 酢酸イソアミル（さくさんイソアミル）and カプロン酸エチル.

**例文**
この本醸造酒は麹歩合を高めにしているが、ほのかな吟醸香もあって軽やかだ。
||This premium sake, made with a small addition of distilled alcohol, uses a higher-than-usual share of kōji rice, yet it is light and has a faint fruity aroma like that of carefully cold-fermented, highly polished sake.||
