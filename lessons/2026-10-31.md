**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月31日**
**漢字**
- 蒸 — 音読み: ジョウ / 訓読み: む-す、む-れる、む-らす — to steam. Sake rice is steamed, never boiled: steam softens the starch so kōji and enzymes can get to work, while each grain stays separate and fairly dry. Read ジョウ in 蒸気（じょうき）, steam, and in 蒸留（じょうりゅう）, distillation.
- 甑 — 音読み: ソウ / 訓読み: こしき — the traditional rice steamer. A rare kanji you'll almost only meet in brewing and archaeology, but in a sake brewery it's everywhere.

**ことば**
- 蒸米（むしまい） — steamed rice for brewing. Every grain that goes into sake passes through this stage, whether it becomes kōji, starter, or one of the mash additions. Note the 訓読み here: むしまい, not じょうまい.
- 甑（こしき） — the big steaming tub, traditionally wooden, now often stainless steel. It sits on top of a 和釜（わがま）, an iron cauldron of boiling water, and steam rises up through the rice.
- 外硬内軟（がいこうないなん） — "firm outside, soft inside": the ideal texture for 蒸米. The surface is dry and springy, so the grains don't stick together, but the core is fully cooked. Brewers check it by kneading a pinch into a flat cake, 捻（ひね）り餅（もち）.
- 放冷（ほうれい） — cooling the steamed rice. It's spread on cloth or sent through a cooling machine, 放冷機（ほうれいき）, down to the temperature each use needs, warmer for kōji and cooler for the mash.

**例文**
甑（こしき）で蒸（む）し上（あ）げた蒸米（むしまい）が外硬内軟（がいこうないなん）に仕上（しあ）がっているか確（たし）かめてから、放冷（ほうれい）して麹室（こうじむろ）へ運（はこ）ぶ。
||After checking that the rice steamed in the koshiki has come out firm outside and soft inside, we cool it and carry it to the kōji room.||
