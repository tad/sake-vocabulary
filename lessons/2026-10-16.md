**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月16日**
**漢字**
- 泡 — 音読み: ホウ / 訓読み: あわ — foam, bubble; the moromi's foam rises and falls in recognizable stages, and brewers read it as a gauge of fermentation (高泡, 泡なし酵母).
- 櫂 — 音読み: トウ / 訓読み: かい — oar, paddle; in the kura it's the long wooden pole used to stir starters and mash (櫂入れ, 櫂棒).
**ことば**
- 並行複発酵（へいこうふくはっこう） — multiple parallel fermentation. Kōji enzymes turn starch into sugar while yeast turns that sugar into alcohol, both in the same tank at the same time; this is why sake can reach the high alcohol it does.
- 櫂入れ（かいいれ） — stirring the starter or mash with the paddle. Evens out temperature, mixes rice and kōji, and feeds the yeast oxygen; how often and how hard is a brewer's judgment call.
- 高泡（たかあわ） — the "tall foam" stage, roughly a week into the moromi, when thick foam piles up high above the mash at peak fermentation. Its height and texture tell the brewer how vigorous things are.
- 泡消し機（あわけしき） — foam-breaker: a rotating device fitted at the tank rim that knocks down foam so it doesn't spill over. 泡なし酵母（あわなしこうぼ）, a low-foam yeast, removes much of the need for it.
**例文**
並行複発酵が進むと醪は高泡を迎え、蔵人（くらびと）は泡消し機を付けて櫂入れを続けた。
||As the multiple parallel fermentation progressed, the moromi reached its tall-foam stage, so the brewery workers fitted a foam-breaker and kept stirring the mash with their paddles.||
