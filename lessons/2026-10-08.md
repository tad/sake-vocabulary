**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月8日**
**漢字**
- 釜 — 音読み: フ / 訓読み: かま — cauldron, kettle; it boils the water under the 甑 that makes the steam. Common sake word: 和釜（わがま）.
- 捻 — 音読み: ネン / 訓読み: ひね-る — to twist, to pinch; this is the hand check brewers give freshly steamed rice. Common sake word: 捻り餅（ひねりもち）.
**ことば**
- 和釜（わがま） — the traditional cauldron set beneath the 甑. Many kura still prize it, and its steam is often described as drier and stronger than boiler steam.
- 連続蒸米機（れんぞくじょうまいき） — continuous steamer: rice rides a mesh belt through a steam tunnel. It's common at larger breweries. Note that 蒸 is read じょう here.
- 捻り餅（ひねりもち） — a pinch of steamed rice kneaded and pressed flat between the fingers to judge its elasticity and stickiness, that is, whether it reached 外硬内軟.
- 掛米（かけまい） — steamed rice added straight into the moromi during the additions, as opposed to rice made into kōji. It's the bulk of the rice in a batch.
**例文**
和釜（わがま）で蒸し上げた掛米（かけまい）を少し取り、捻り餅（ひねりもち）にして蒸し具合を確かめる。
||They take a little of the mash-addition rice steamed over the traditional cauldron, pinch and knead it into a small flat cake, and check how well it's steamed.||
