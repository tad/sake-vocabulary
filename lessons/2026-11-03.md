**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月3日**
**漢字**
- 床 — 音読み: ショウ / 訓読み: とこ、ゆか — bed, floor. In the 麹室（こうじむろ） it's the long wooden work table, often cloth-covered, where the rice is spread, spored and kneaded. Brewers often call that table just 床（とこ）.
- 揉 — 音読み: ジュウ / 訓読み: も-む — to rub, to knead (not a 常用 kanji, so it's often written in kana: もむ). In the 蔵（くら） it's the hand-kneading that works kōji spores into the rice, as in 床揉み（とこもみ）.

**ことば**
- 引き込み（ひきこみ） — carrying the cooled steamed rice into the 麹室（こうじむろ）: the first step of 製麹（せいぎく）. Also written 引込み.
- 種切り（たねきり） — sprinkling the 種麹（たねこうじ） over the rice spread on the 床（とこ）, usually from a fine sieve-like shaker so the spores fall evenly.
- 床揉み（とこもみ） — rubbing the spores into the rice by hand right after 種切り（たねきり）, so every grain is coated. The rice is then heaped and wrapped in cloth to keep warm. Often written 床もみ.
- 切り返し（きりかえし） — roughly half a day later the rice has warmed and clumped. The team unwraps it, breaks up the clumps to even out temperature and moisture, and wraps it again.

**例文**
引き込み（ひきこみ）を終（お）えた蒸米（むしまい）を床（とこ）に広（ひろ）げ、種切り（たねきり）と床揉み（とこもみ）をしたら、半日（はんにち）ほどで切り返し（きりかえし）をする。
||The steamed rice, now carried into the kōji room, is spread out on the bed; once the spores have been sprinkled on and rubbed in, the clumps are broken up and remixed about half a day later.||
