**漢字**
- 泡 — 音読み: ホウ / 訓読み: あわ — foam, bubble. After the 留添（とめぞえ）, the 醪（もろみ） foams up in recognizable stages, and brewers read the foam as a gauge of how fermentation is going (高泡（たかあわ）, 泡なし酵母（あわなしこうぼ）).
- 複 — 音読み: フク / 訓読み: （常用の訓読みなし） — multiple, compound, double (as in 複雑（ふくざつ）, complicated). In sake it's the 複 of 並行複発酵（へいこうふくはっこう）, the double fermentation that makes sake special.

**ことば**
- 並行複発酵（へいこうふくはっこう） — multiple parallel fermentation. Kōji enzymes turn starch into sugar (糖化（とうか）, saccharification) while yeast turns that sugar into alcohol, both at the same time in the same tank. Beer does the two steps one after the other (単行複発酵（たんこうふくはっこう）). Doing them together is why sake can reach around 20% alcohol.
- 高泡（たかあわ） — "tall foam": the stage a few days to about a week into the mash, when thick, sticky foam piles up high above the surface at the peak of fermentation. Its height and texture tell the brewer how vigorous the yeast is.
- 泡消し機（あわけしき） — foam breaker: a rotating device fitted at the rim of the tank that knocks the foam down so it doesn't spill over.
- 泡なし酵母（あわなしこうぼ） — low-foam yeast: strains selected so they don't form tall foam (for example 協会（きょうかい）701号, the foamless version of No. 7). The tank can be filled fuller, and the brewer saves the work of watching and cleaning foam.

**例文**
並行複発酵（へいこうふくはっこう）が盛（さか）んになると醪（もろみ）は高泡（たかあわ）を迎え、泡（あわ）が溢（あふ）れないように蔵人（くらびと）は泡消し機（あわけしき）を取（と）り付（つ）ける。
||When parallel fermentation gets going in earnest, the mash reaches its tall-foam stage, and the brewery workers fit a foam breaker so the foam doesn't overflow.||
