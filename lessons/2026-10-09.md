**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月9日**
**漢字**
- 麹 — 音読み: キク / 訓読み: こうじ — kōji: steamed rice grown through with mold. It's the heart of the craft, and the brewery saying goes 一麹、二酛、三造り（いちこうじ、にもと、さんづくり）: kōji first, the starter second, the mash third. Seen in 麹米（こうじまい）, the share of rice that becomes kōji. You'll also meet the native-Japanese character 糀（こうじ） on some labels and kōji products.
- 室 — 音読み: シツ / 訓読み: むろ — room, chamber; specifically a warm, sealed room. In the kura it nearly always means the kōji room. The word: 麹室.

**ことば**
- 麹室（こうじむろ） — the kōji room: an insulated, warm, humid room, traditionally lined with cedar, where the kōji is grown over about two days. Brewers often just call it 室.
- 種麹（たねこうじ） — kōji starter: dried spores of 麹菌（こうじきん） (*Aspergillus oryzae*). Kura buy it from specialist suppliers called 種麹屋（たねこうじや）, and only a handful of these remain in Japan.
- 引き込み（ひきこみ） — carrying the cooled steamed rice into the 麹室. This is the first step of 製麹（せいぎく）, kōji-making. The rice is spread on a cloth-covered bed (床（とこ）) to even out its temperature.
- 種切り（たねきり） — sprinkling the 種麹 over the steamed rice. It's done from a small sieve-like shaker, high above the rice so the spores fall as a fine, even dusting.

**例文**
引き込みを終えた蒸米を床に広げ、温度が落ち着いたところで種切りをする。麹室の中は、むっとするほど暖かい。
||Once the steamed rice has been brought in, it's spread on the bed, and when its temperature has settled, the kōji spores are sprinkled on. Inside the kōji room it's stiflingly warm.||
