**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月21日**
**漢字**
- 貯 — 音読み: チョ / 訓読み: たくわ-える — to store, save up; after pressing and pasteurizing, sake rests in tanks for months before release. 貯蔵（ちょぞう）, storage, is the everyday sake word.
- 詰 — 音読み: キツ / 訓読み: つ-める、つ-まる — to pack, fill, stuff; in sake it means filling bottles or tanks, as in 瓶詰め（びんづめ）, bottling, and in the pasteurization label terms below.
**ことば**
- 火入れ（ひいれ） — pasteurization. Sake is gently heated to about 60-65°C, which kills spoilage bacteria and stops leftover kōji enzymes. Standard practice is twice: once after pressing before storage, and once at bottling.
- 火落ち（ひおち） — spoilage by 火落菌（ひおちきん）, alcohol-tolerant lactic acid bacteria that turn sake cloudy, sour and off-smelling. Preventing it is the original reason for 火入れ.
- 生酒（なまざけ） — sake that has never been pasteurized. Fresh and lively, but it must be kept refrigerated; also called 本生（ほんなま）.
- 生詰め（なまづめ） — pasteurized once before storage, but not again at bottling. The classic autumn style ひやおろし is a 生詰め sake.
- 生貯蔵酒（なまちょぞうしゅ） — the reverse: stored unpasteurized, then pasteurized once at bottling. Easy to confuse with 生詰め, so check which half of the process the 生 refers to.
**例文**
春に一度火入れして貯蔵（ちょぞう）した酒を、秋に生詰めのまま瓶詰めして出荷（しゅっか）する。
||The sake is pasteurized once in spring and put into storage, then in autumn it is bottled without a second pasteurization and shipped.||
