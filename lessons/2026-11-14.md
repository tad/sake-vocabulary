**漢字**
- 滓 — 音読み: シ / 訓読み: おり、かす — dregs, sediment (not a 常用 kanji). After pressing, fine bits of rice, kōji and yeast slip through the bags and settle in the tank; in the kura this sediment is read おり: 滓引き（おりびき）, 滓がらみ（おりがらみ）.
- 濾 — 音読み: ロ / 訓読み: こ-す — to filter, to strain (not a 常用 kanji). The key kanji of 濾過（ろか）, filtration, and of the label word 無濾過（むろか）. Because it's hard to write, print often mixes kana in: ろ過.

**ことば**
- 滓（おり） — the sediment that settles out of freshly pressed sake. Also written 澱（おり）, and on labels often just おり in kana.
- 滓引き（おりびき） — racking: the freshly pressed sake rests about a week to ten days, then the clear sake on top is drawn off into another tank, leaving the 滓 behind.
- 濾過（ろか） — filtration, the step after 滓引き. It removes finer particles and, traditionally with charcoal, also adjusts color and flavor.
- 無濾過（むろか） — "unfiltered" on a label. Usually it means no charcoal filtration, not no cleanup at all: the sake was still racked and is normally clear, not cloudy.
- 滓がらみ（おりがらみ） — lightly hazy sake bottled with a little of the 滓 left in. Also written おりがらみ; much lighter than fully cloudy sake.

**例文**
搾（しぼ）った酒を数日（すうじつ）休ませて滓引きをしてから、濾過をしない無濾過の酒と、滓を少し残した滓がらみの酒を別の瓶（びん）に詰（つ）めた。
||After resting the freshly pressed sake for a few days and racking it off the sediment, we bottled an unfiltered sake and a lightly hazy sake with a little sediment left in, in separate bottles.||
