**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月2日**
**漢字**
- 麹 — 音読み: キク / 訓読み: こうじ — kōji: steamed rice grown through with mold. It's the heart of the craft; the old brewery saying is 一麹（いちこうじ）、二酛（にもと）、三造（さんづく）り: kōji first, the starter second, the mash third. You've already met it in 麹米（こうじまい）. Some labels and kōji products use the native-Japanese character 糀（こうじ） instead.
- 室 — 音読み: シツ / 訓読み: むろ — room, chamber; as むろ, a warm, sealed room. In the 蔵（くら） it nearly always means the kōji room: 麹室（こうじむろ）.

**ことば**
- 麹室（こうじむろ） — the kōji room: insulated, warm and humid, traditionally lined with 杉（すぎ） (cedar). Brewers usually just call it 室（むろ）.
- 麹菌（こうじきん） — the kōji mold, *Aspergillus oryzae*. Its enzymes turn rice starch into sugar for the yeast to ferment. In 2006 the Brewing Society of Japan named it Japan's "national fungus", 国菌（こっきん）.
- 種麹（たねこうじ） — kōji starter: dried spores of 麹菌（こうじきん）. Breweries buy it from specialist suppliers called 種麹屋（たねこうじや）, and only a handful of these remain in Japan.
- 製麹（せいぎく） — kōji-making: the roughly two-day process in the 麹室（こうじむろ）. Note the reading: 麹 is ぎく here (its 音読み キク, voiced), not こうじ.

**例文**
蔵人（くらびと）は放冷（ほうれい）した蒸米（むしまい）を麹室（こうじむろ）に運（はこ）び、種麹（たねこうじ）を振（ふ）りかけて、二日かけて麹菌（こうじきん）を育てる。
||The brewery workers carry the cooled steamed rice into the kōji room, sprinkle it with kōji starter, and spend two days growing the kōji mold.||
