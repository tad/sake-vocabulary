**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月13日**

**漢字**
- 粕 — 音読み: ハク / 訓読み: かす — lees, dregs (not a 常用 kanji). Whatever stays behind in the bag after pressing is 粕: the word is 酒粕（さけかす）, sake lees. On its own the on-reading ハク is almost never used; read it かす.
- 責 — 音読み: セキ / 訓読み: せ-める — to press hard, to torment; responsibility. You may know it from 責任（せきにん）. In the press it names the last, hardest-squeezed portion of sake: 責め（せめ）.

**ことば**
- 荒走り（あらばしり） — the first sake to run from the press, before any real pressure is applied. Usually a little cloudy, lively and fresh, sometimes slightly fizzy. Often bottled separately and sold under this name in winter.
- 中取り（なかどり） — the middle run: clear, balanced, considered the best part. Breweries often choose it for competition sake. Also called 中汲み（なかぐみ）.
- 責め（せめ） — the last run, pressed out under the strongest pressure. Heavier and with more rough edges (雑味（ざつみ）), it is usually blended rather than bottled alone.
- 酒粕（さけかす） — sake lees: the white cake of rice, kōji and yeast left after pressing. Pressed into sheets it is 板粕（いたかす）; in the kitchen it becomes 粕汁（かすじる）, lees soup, and a kind of 甘酒（あまざけ）.

**例文**
槽搾り（ふねしぼり）では荒走り、中取り、責めの順（じゅん）に酒が出てきて、酒袋（さかぶくろ）の中には酒粕が残る。
||In box pressing, the sake comes out as the first run, then the middle run, then the hard-pressed last run, and the sake lees stay behind in the pressing bags.||
