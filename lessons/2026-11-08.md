**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月8日**
**漢字**
- 樽 — 音読み: ソン / 訓読み: たる — cask, barrel. You'll know it from 樽酒（たるざけ）, sake aged briefly in cedar casks, but inside the kura it also names barrel-shaped tools, like today's 暖気樽（だきだる）.
- 湧 — 音読み: ユウ / 訓読み: わ-く — to well up, to gush (as a spring does). Brewers use it for a starter that comes alive with bubbling fermentation: 湧き付き（わきつき）.

**ことば**
- 打瀬（うたせ） — the cold holding period of a traditional 生酛 or 山廃 starter. For a week or two the starter rests at a low temperature while natural lactic acid bacteria make acid and keep unwanted microbes out, before any warming begins.
- 暖気樽（だきだる） — a small barrel filled with hot water and set into the starter to warm it gently, step by step, on a daily schedule. Note the special reading of 暖気 as だき. Today it's often stainless steel rather than wood.
- 湧き付き（わきつき） — the point when the yeast takes over and the starter starts fermenting in earnest: bubbles, rising temperature, a fresh fermenting aroma. The verb form is 湧き付く（わきつく）.
- 分け（わけ） — near the end, dividing the starter into smaller vessels to cool it quickly and calm the fermentation, so the yeast stays healthy until the starter goes into the main mash.

**例文**
打瀬（うたせ）が終わった酛（もと）を暖気樽（だきだる）で少しずつ温（あたた）めていくと、やがて湧き付き（わきつき）を迎え、最後に分け（わけ）で冷（ひ）やされる。
||Once its cold resting period is over, the starter is warmed little by little with a hot-water barrel until it finally springs into active fermentation, and at the end it is split into smaller vessels to cool down.||
