**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月15日**
**漢字**
- 醪 — 音読み: ロウ / 訓読み: もろみ — fermenting mash; the main fermentation of rice, kōji, water and yeast, built on top of the finished starter. Today we leave the 酛 behind and enter the tank: 醪（もろみ）.
- 添 — 音読み: テン / 訓読み: そ-える、そ-う — to add, to accompany. In the kura it names each addition of rice, kōji and water to the mash, as in 初添（はつぞえ）.

**ことば**
- 三段仕込み（さんだんじこみ） — three-stage mashing. Rice, kōji and water go into the tank in three batches over four days, roughly doubling the volume each time, so the yeast is never diluted faster than it can multiply and the acidity stays protective. The standard method in nearly every kura.
- 初添（はつぞえ） — the first addition. The starter is moved to the tank and the first, smallest batch is added, kept relatively warm (around 12°C) so the yeast gets going. Often shortened to 添（そえ）.
- 踊り（おどり） — "the dance": the rest day after 初添. Nothing is added; the yeast multiplies and the mash settles. Brewers read the mash's condition this day before going on.
- 仲添（なかぞえ） — the second, middle addition on day three, roughly twice the first, at a lower temperature (around 9–10°C). Also just 仲（なか）.
- 留添（とめぞえ） — the third and final addition on day four, the largest batch, mashed cold (around 7–8°C). Also 留（とめ）. After this the 醪 ferments on its own for several weeks.

**例文**
初添の翌日は踊りといって仕込みを休み、酵母が増えるのを待ってから仲添、留添と進めます。
||The day after the first addition is the "dance" rest day: nothing new goes in, and the brewers wait for the yeast to multiply before moving on to the middle addition and then the final one.||
