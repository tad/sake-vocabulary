**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月19日**
**漢字**
- 滓 — 音読み: シ、サイ / 訓読み: おり、かす — dregs, sediment; in sake, the fine cloudy particles of rice and yeast that slip through the press and settle in the tank. Read おり in the kura: 滓引き（おりびき）racking off the sediment, and labels like 滓がらみ（おりがらみ）, lightly cloudy sake bottled with some of it.
- 濾 — 音読み: ロ / 訓読み: こ-す — to filter, strain. The key kanji of 濾過（ろか）filtration, the step after settling, and of the label term 無濾過（むろか）. Often simplified to ろ過 in print.
**ことば**
- 荒走り（あらばしり） — the first sake to run from the press before real pressure is applied. Often a little cloudy, lively and rough-edged; some kura bottle it separately under this name.
- 中取り（なかどり） — the middle run of the pressing, after 荒走り and before full pressure. Prized as the most balanced and clear portion; also called 中汲み（なかぐみ）, and often the part sent to competitions.
- 責め（せめ） — the last run, squeezed out under the heaviest pressure. Firmer, more bitter and astringent; usually blended back in rather than bottled alone.
- 滓引き（おりびき） — letting freshly pressed sake rest about a week to ten days so the sediment settles, then drawing the clear sake off the top into another tank.
- 無濾過（むろか） — "unfiltered" on a label: no charcoal filtration (炭濾過（すみろか）), so more color and flavor are left in. It usually still means the sake was settled and coarse-filtered, so it isn't necessarily cloudy.
**例文**
荒走りと責めは別のタンクに分け、中取りだけを滓引きしてから、無濾過のまま瓶詰めした。
||We set aside the first free-run and the hard-pressed final run in separate tanks, racked only the middle run off its sediment, and bottled it without charcoal filtering.||
