**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 10月14日**
**漢字**
- 暖 — 音読み: ダン / 訓読み: あたたかい・あたためる — warm; to warm. In the starter room it takes the special reading だき in 暖気（だき）, the controlled warming that wakes a 生酛 or 山廃 starter up.
- 樽 — 音読み: ソン / 訓読み: たる — cask, barrel. Familiar from 樽酒（たるざけ）, cedar-cask sake, but in the kura it also names small wooden or metal barrels used as tools, like today's 暖気樽.
**ことば**
- 打瀬（うたせ） — the cold holding period in a traditional starter, after the mash is made (and, for 生酛, ground). For several days to a couple of weeks it sits at low temperature while natural lactic acid bacteria do their work, before any warming begins.
- 暖気樽（だきだる） — a small barrel filled with hot water and lowered into the starter to raise its temperature step by step. Brewers move it in and out, and stir around it, on a careful daily schedule; today it's often stainless steel rather than wood.
- 湧き付き（わきつき） — the moment the yeast takes over and the starter begins to ferment in earnest: bubbles, rising temperature, a fresh fermenting aroma. It's the sign that the starter is alive and on track.
- 分け（わけ） — late in the starter's life, dividing it into smaller vessels to cool it quickly and calm the fermentation, so the yeast stays healthy until the starter is used to build the main mash.
**例文**
打瀬の間は酛を低温に保ち、その後、暖気樽で少しずつ温めていくと、やがて湧き付きを迎える。
||During the cold holding period the starter is kept at a low temperature; then, as it is warmed little by little with a hot-water barrel, it eventually reaches the point where the yeast begins fermenting vigorously.||
