**🍶 今日の酒ことば — 11月1日**
**漢字**
- 掛 — 音読み: カイ (rarely used) / 訓読み: か-ける、か-かる — to hang, to put on, to add. In the brewery it marks the rice that is "added" straight into the starter and mash. Common sake word: 掛米（かけまい）.
- 倒 — 音読み: トウ / 訓読み: たお-れる、たお-す — to fall over, to topple. Its sake moment is the end of the steaming season, when the steamer is symbolically "toppled": 甑倒（こしきだお）し.

**ことば**
- 麹米（こうじまい） — rice that will be made into kōji, roughly a fifth to a quarter of all the rice in a batch. Brewers often give it their best, most highly polished rice, and some labels list it separately from the 掛米.
- 掛米（かけまい） — steamed rice that goes straight into the starter and the mash instead of becoming kōji. It's the bulk of the rice in a batch.
- 連続蒸米機（れんぞくじょうまいき） — continuous rice steamer: the rice rides a mesh belt through a tunnel of steam. Common at larger breweries. Note that 蒸 is read じょう here, unlike むし in 蒸米.
- 甑倒（こしきだお）し — "toppling the koshiki": the last steaming of the brewing 季節（きせつ）. Once the steamer isn't needed any more, the hardest part of the season is over, and the 蔵（くら） usually celebrates with a small party.

**例文**
小さな蔵（くら）では麹米（こうじまい）と掛米（かけまい）を毎朝（まいあさ）甑（こしき）で蒸（む）し、最後の蒸しが終（お）わる甑倒（こしきだお）しの日には、蔵人（くらびと）みんなで乾杯（かんぱい）する。
||At a small brewery they steam the kōji rice and the mash rice in the koshiki every morning, and on the day of the last steaming, the whole brewing crew raises a toast together.||
